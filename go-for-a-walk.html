How it works

01

Set your distance or time

Want to walk 3 kilometers? Only have 45 minutes? Choose distance or duration—whatever fits your day.

02

Customize your walk

Parks, coffee stops, dog parks, landmarks, playgrounds—tap what you want to discover along the way. Prefer flat or hilly? You choose.

03

Pick a route and go

Choose from three unique loop routes, each heading a different direction. Follow voice-guided directions so your phone can stay in your pocket.

🚶

Personalized to your pace. Connect Apple Health and we'll use your actual walking speed to estimate how long each route will take—so "45 minutes" means 45 minutes for you.
