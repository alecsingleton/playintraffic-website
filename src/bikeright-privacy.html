Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

The Short Version

We never see your location or your rides.

BikeRight has no account, no ads, and no analytics. To build and score routes it asks a few mapping services for data about the streets around you, and those requests include coordinates but nothing that identifies you. Your rides, saved places, and settings stay on your device. Play in Traffic doesn't run servers that receive any of it.

What We Don't Collect

Play in Traffic does not receive or store any of the following:

  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Routes you generate or ride
  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Fitness or health information
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs that identify you

There are currently no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising services active in BikeRight. If we ever turn on anonymous analytics, we'll update this policy first and give you a switch to turn it off in Settings.

How the App Works

Here's what happens when you use BikeRight:

  • Your device's GPS determines your current location on your phone or watch
  • To find the bike infrastructure near you, the app requests OpenStreetMap data for the surrounding area (a map rectangle, rounded to roughly 100 m) from the public Overpass API
  • To build turn-by-turn routes, it sends start, destination, and waypoint coordinates to Google's Directions and Roads APIs
  • If you type an address, Apple's address search turns it into a location
  • Safety scoring, route ranking, and your ride history are handled on your device
  • None of these requests carry your name, an account, or a device identifier, and nothing about your location, routes, or rides is sent to Play in Traffic (we don't have servers for it)

No Account Required—Ever

BikeRight does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app. There is no login, no profile, and no cloud sync. You open the app, you ride, you're done.

Third-Party Services

BikeRight relies on these services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • OpenStreetMap data via the Overpass API: receives the map area it's asked about. OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps Platform (directions and road matching): receives route coordinates. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple (maps display and address search). Apple Privacy Policy

BikeRight does not use any advertising or data broker services.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your preferences, saved places, saved routes, and ride history are stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage. This data:

  • Is never uploaded to Play in Traffic
  • Is protected by your device's built-in security and encryption
  • Is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app
  • Can be cleared at any time within app settings

Location Permission

BikeRight requests access to your device's location in order to generate routes starting from where you are. When you grant this permission:

  • Your location is used to fetch nearby map data and build routes, as described above
  • It is never sent to Play in Traffic
  • You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services
  • If you deny location access, you can still enter a starting point manually

Children's Privacy

BikeRight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

Our commitment is simple: we will never start collecting your data without your explicit, informed consent. If this policy ever changes:

  • We will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page
  • We will notify you within the app before any change takes effect
  • We will never collect data retroactively
