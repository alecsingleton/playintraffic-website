About Play in Traffic

The name

We named the company Play in Traffic because that's literally what our apps help you do — run, ride, and walk through your city, safely. It started as a joke that stuck, and honestly it describes the mission better than anything polished would.

Who's building this

Play in Traffic is a one-person project. The person behind it has spent years working in health tech and wearable products — building things that help people move more and stress less. This is the same idea, just without a boardroom.

Where we are

Three apps, all iOS, all free. Random Run for runners, BikeRight for cyclists, and Go for a Walk for everyone. Each one is built around the same convictions: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your location stays on your phone. That's not a feature — it's just how things should work.
